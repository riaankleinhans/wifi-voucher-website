Happy WiFi — terms between Happy WiFi and the kit owner (the person or business that buys and runs the kit).

This document explains who owns what and what each party is responsible for.

By buying, receiving, or using a Happy WiFi kit, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, do not use the kit.

Support: hello@happywifi.co.za · WhatsApp 072 896 1895

1. What this is

A Happy WiFi kit lets the owner sell timed Wi‑Fi access where they operate, using their own uncapped internet. The kit runs on site (Happy WiFi Modem, Phone sales terminal, and Bluetooth thermal printer). Sales and the customer Wi‑Fi network run on the kit itself — not in a remote cloud service.

The kit owner buys the hardware and loads prepaid selling credit (top-up PIN) to keep selling. All Wi‑Fi sales money stays with the kit owner.

How it works: Self-service — order the kit, set it up yourself using the Getting started sheet, and sell. Kits can be shipped anywhere, including online orders delivered far from Happy WiFi. On-site install or hand-holding is not included unless agreed separately in writing.

3. Kit owner responsibilities

The kit owner agrees to:

  1. Setup — Unpack the kit and follow the Getting started sheet (connect internet, power on, load gift/top-up PIN, set Wi‑Fi name and prices, run a test sale). Setup is the owner’s responsibility unless paid install was agreed in writing.
  2. Internet — Provide and pay for working uncapped internet and an ISP modem, with Ethernet to the Happy WiFi Modem as shown in Getting started.
  3. Selling — Make sure whoever sells can use the Phone sales terminal (take payment → tap product → give slip). The owner handles all customer money, change, and disputes.
  4. Power — Keep the Happy WiFi Modem plugged in. The Phone sales terminal and printer can run on battery, but batteries must stay charged so selling is not interrupted.
  5. Printer paper — Keep 58 mm rolls loaded. Starter paper ships with the kit; ongoing paper is the owner’s cost.
  6. Selling credit — Buy top-up packs as needed and enter each top-up PIN in Sell → Seller's Portal (see §5). Keep enough credit to sell.
  7. Shop details — Keep Wi‑Fi display name, Seller's email, owner contact, and prices up to date in Seller's Portal.
  8. Physical care & replacement — Protect hardware from theft, damage, and tampering. Replace or repair lost or broken items at owner cost so selling can continue.
  9. Do not reset — Do not factory-reset, wipe, or “fix” the Phone sales terminal without Happy WiFi’s instruction. Unauthorized changes can stop it working for selling and may lose sales history.
  10. Legal use — Operate lawfully at the location and follow rules for offering internet to customers in your area. The owner must stay within their ISP’s terms (including whether reselling or sharing access is allowed).
  11. Reports — Provide a working Seller's email and read credit / lock warnings in the app.

Happy WiFi is not responsible when selling stops because: no sales were made; batteries were flat; the internet line was down; or selling credit ran out without a new top-up (see §5).

4. Happy WiFi responsibilities

Under the self-service model in §1, Happy WiFi agrees to:

  1. Supply — Ship the complete kit as ordered (Happy WiFi Modem, Phone sales terminal, Bluetooth thermal printer, cables, chargers, starter paper, and Getting started materials), prepared and tested before dispatch.
  2. Documentation — Provide Getting started and operator guides in the box and on happywifi.co.za.
  3. Selling credit — After payment for a top-up pack, issue a 12-digit kit-bound top-up PIN (WhatsApp, SMS, email, or shop portal when live).
  4. Updates and service — Keep kits working: software updates on the Phone sales terminal, automatic sales reports, top-up PIN support, and remote fixes when needed. Updates move forward only — older app versions cannot be restored.
  5. Remote support — Best-effort help by email and WhatsApp during normal business hours for kit problems (selling locked / no credit, printer issues, Phone sales terminal not connecting to the Happy WiFi Modem, app stuck updating). Support is remote only unless on-site work was agreed and paid for separately. No guaranteed response time unless a separate written agreement says so.

7. Hardware failure and replacement

  • The kit owner arranges repair or replacement at owner cost, except where a manufacturer warranty applies.
  • Happy WiFi can supply and configure replacement hardware as a paid service if agreed.
  • Selling may be unavailable until replacement hardware is set up and selling credit is loaded.

8. Email reports and data

  • The kit sends daily (yesterday’s sales) and monthly summary emails automatically.
  • Seller's email receives sales-only reports. Happy WiFi may receive fuller detail (including usage, device versions, and last-known location) to support the kit.
  • Providing Seller's email in Seller's Portal counts as consent to this reporting.

9. Acceptance

The kit is accepted when the owner finishes Getting started: kit connected and powered, gift/top-up PIN loaded, test sale works, Wi‑Fi name and prices are set. Report problems within 7 days of first setup; after that, issues are handled as remote support under §4 and §6.

10. Changes

Happy WiFi may update the app, service limits, or these terms. Pack price changes follow §5. Other material changes will be communicated before they apply. Buying a new top-up or continuing to sell after notice means acceptance unless the owner stops topping up and stops selling.
