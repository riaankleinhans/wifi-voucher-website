If you run a shop

If you can run a shop, you can sell Happy WiFi

You own the kit. You keep every WiFi sale. You already have internet. If you can run a shop, you can sell this.

A normal day

  1. Remember to keep the Phone sales terminal and the printer charged — sales will not work if either battery is flat.
  2. Ask what they want to buy (time or data — matching how your shop sells) → take their money → on the Phone sales terminal tap the correct button → tap Yes → give them the slip.
  3. No paper? Tap Reprint last on Sell. Changed their mind? Tap Sales, match the slip to the sale, then Void.

When the box arrives

Follow the one Getting started guide: power on the Happy WiFi router, connect the network cable, switch on the printer and Phone sales terminal, then finish setup in Seller's Portal (gift/top-up PIN and shop owner PIN from the box, then shop name, email, Sell by Time or Data, and prices). Your shop name becomes your shop WiFi.

Short counter reminder: How to sell.

When credit runs low

Buy a top-up at happywifi.co.za/top-up, then enter the top-up PIN in Seller's Portal (bottom of Sell) under Selling credit. Credit once loaded does not expire. Top-ups fund software and support — WiFi sales stay yours.
