In the shop

Pay at the counter. Go online.

Happy WiFi is an internet re-selling kit for your shop. You sell time or data on the internet you already have. Customers pay you, get a slip, and go online — easier than selling airtime.

What you need

  • Your own uncapped internet (whoever you already use).
  • A Happy WiFi router
  • A Phone sales terminal at the counter
  • A small printer
  • Selling credit (top-up PIN) that keeps the software going — and helps more shops offer affordable WiFi.

Your shop WiFi

  • Customers join your shop WiFi.
  • There is no password to join.
  • They only get internet after they use the code you sold them.

The money

What they pay you

That is yours. Charge what makes sense for your area. Change prices any time on the Phone sales terminal.

Selling credit

You buy top-up packs when you need them. After you pay, we send a PIN for your Kit ID. Load it in Seller's Portal — credit stays until you sell it down. What customers pay you stays in your pocket.

What you can sell

In Seller's Portal you choose Sell by Time or Sell by Data. Same buttons either way — only the labels and what the customer gets change.

Time: short times can be used in pieces — if they step outside, the clock pauses until they come back. A day starts when they first go online, then runs until the same time tomorrow.

Data: buttons show data sizes (for example 200 MB up to a few GB). Each code also ends after 7 days even if data remains.

One phone or laptop per slip. You choose which packages to offer and what they cost.

Customer reward

Reward loyal customers with free WiFi when they spend in your store (about 15 minutes on Time, or a small data thank-you on Data). They learn about your WiFi, then come back for more.
