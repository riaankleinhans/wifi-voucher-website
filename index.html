Every sale is yours

Use the uncapped line you already pay for. Happy WiFi helps you sell time or data on it, from your counter.

Easier than airtime

  1. Customer chooses a package (time or data — you pick how your shop sells) and pays you.
  2. You tap that button on the Phone sales terminal. A slip prints with a code and a QR.
  3. They join your shop WiFi, scan, and they are online.

You choose the selling prices and whether to sell by time or by data. Email us when you are ready to start printing money.
