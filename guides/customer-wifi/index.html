Some shops sell by data (MB / GB) instead — the till board or staff will tell you.

Then

  1. Pay at the counter.
  2. Keep the slip — code and QR are on it.
  3. Join your shop WiFi — no password.
  4. Scan the QR. If it does not work, the slip has a web address and the code.
  5. You are online — WhatsApp, Chrome, YouTube as usual.

If you bought time

Short times only count while you are online. Step out, come back, finish what you paid for. A day starts when you first go online, then runs until the same time tomorrow.

If you bought data

Use the data on the slip. It also ends after 7 days even if data remains.

Stuck? Ask at the counter. One phone or laptop per slip.
