How to sell

Use the Phone sales terminal. Remember to keep it and the printer charged — sales will not work if either battery is flat.

Sell

  1. Ask what they want to buy (time or data — matching how your shop sells).
  2. Take the customer's money.
  3. On the Phone sales terminal, tap the correct button.
  4. Tap Yes. Give them the slip.

Also remember

  1. No paper came out → tap Reprint last on Sell. Do not sell again.
  2. Void a sale → tap Sales → match the slip to the sale in the list → tap Void. You cannot undo Void.
  3. Tell the customer: join your shop WiFi (no password) → scan the QR. If the scan fails, the slip has a web address and the code.
  4. When credit runs low: buy a top-up from Happy WiFi, open Seller's Portal (bottom of Sell), and enter the new top-up PIN under Selling credit.
First day setup is on the Getting started sheet in the box (also online under Shop guides). Stuck? Email hello@happywifi.co.za or WhatsApp 072 896 1895. Do not wipe or reset the Phone sales terminal.
