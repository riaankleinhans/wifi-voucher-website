Top-up

Thank you

If PayFast confirmed your payment, we will email your 12-digit top-up PIN to the address you used at checkout. Load it on the kit before the date in that email (usually within 14 days of issue).

Phase 1: PINs are issued by Happy WiFi after we see the payment — not instantly on this page. Same day is typical on business days; WhatsApp us if you need it sooner.

Load the PIN

  1. Open Sell → Seller's Portal on the Phone sales terminal.
  2. Under Selling credit, enter the PIN (dashes appear as you type).
  3. Credit once loaded does not expire. Keep selling.
